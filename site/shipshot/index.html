How it works

  1. Point it at something

    A live site, a local build on localhost, a desktop app made with Electron, or any program on your PC. Save the ones you capture often and they're one click away.

  2. Let it explore

    Shipshot works through every page in four tabs at once, opens tabs, menus and dialogs, scrolls long pages a screen at a time, and takes phone-size shots. Most sites take a minute or two.

  3. Copy and post

    Everything lands in your Library: ready-to-post images, every screen and a gallery. Copy pastes straight into X or Discord, Save drops a file wherever you want it.

Safe to point at your real app.

Shipshot only looks. It's built so a tour of your app can't change anything in it.

  • Never types, never submits. Buttons that act, like save, send, delete, buy, sign in and sign up, are never clicked.
  • Stays on your site. A link or redirect to another site, such as a Google sign-in page, is skipped, not photographed.
  • Desktop apps run as a copy. Shipshot starts a separate copy of the app on a throwaway copy of its settings, so your real window and data are untouched.
  • Everything stays on your PC. No account, no upload. Captures go to a folder you choose.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Shipshot is free to download and use, with no account and no limits.
Does it work on Mac or Linux?
Not yet. Shipshot is built for Windows 10 and 11.
What do I need installed?
Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, and Node.js 20 or newer. The setup file checks for Node.js and opens the download page if it's missing.
Can it capture pages behind a login?
For desktop apps, yes: it works from a copy of the app's saved sign-in. For websites it captures what a signed-out visitor sees, for now.
Where are my screenshots saved?
In Desktop\Screenshots\Shipshot, unless you pick another folder in Settings. Each capture gets its own folder named after the site and the time.
